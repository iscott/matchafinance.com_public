FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Matcha Invoices for?

Matcha Invoices is built for production freelancers, solopreneurs, and small businesses. Anyone who bills clients for their work and wants invoicing to take minutes, not hours.

How fast can I send an invoice?

Pick a client, add your line items and send. Clients and services you have used before are suggested as you type, so a repeat invoice takes only a few clicks.

How do my clients receive an invoice?

By email, with the PDF attached and a button that opens the invoice online. Your client does not need an account or an app to view or download it.

Does Matcha Invoices work on my phone?

Yes. Matcha Invoices runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet and laptop, and you can add it to your home screen to open it full-screen like an app.

Can I see whether a client has opened my invoice?

Yes. Each invoice shows when it was emailed and when your client viewed it, so you know when to follow up.
